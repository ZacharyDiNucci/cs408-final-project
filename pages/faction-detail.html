Imperial Guard

The hammer of the Emperor

Build Army List
Infantry Vehicles Artillery

The Imperial Guard, also known as the Astra Militarum, are the primary fighting force of the Imperium of Man. Comprised of countless billions of soldiers from across the galaxy, they represent humanity's might through sheer numbers, devastating firepower, and unwavering courage. Where a Space Marine conquers, the Imperial Guard holds.

Army Rule: Combined Arms

Infantry units within 6" of a friendly Vehicle unit gain +1 to their hit rolls. Vehicle units within 6" of a friendly Infantry unit gain +1 to their Leadership tests. This represents the tactical synergy between the Guard's infantry and armored divisions working in concert.

Tactical Tip: Keep your infantry squads close to tanks for improved accuracy, while your vehicles benefit from infantry support when morale matters.
