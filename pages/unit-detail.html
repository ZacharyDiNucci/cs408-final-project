Cadian Shock Troops

Imperial Guard Infantry Squad

Add to Army
Infantry Battleline Imperial Guard

The backbone of the Imperial Guard, Cadian Shock Troops are renowned throughout the galaxy for their discipline, courage, and tactical acumen. Raised on the fortress world of Cadia, these soldiers are trained from birth to be the Emperor's finest conventional warriors.

Unit Abilities

Born Soldiers

You can re-roll hit rolls of 1 for models in this unit. The Cadian training regimen is legendary, producing soldiers who rarely miss their mark.

Voice of Command

The Sergeant in this unit can issue orders to nearby Infantry units at the start of your Command phase. Orders include: Take Aim!, Bring it Down!, or Fix Bayonets!

For the Emperor!

Once per battle, when this unit is selected to fight, you can re-roll all hit and wound rolls until the end of the phase. They will not falter in the face of the enemy.
